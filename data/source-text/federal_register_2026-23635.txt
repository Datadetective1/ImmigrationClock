Information Collection Requirement Title: Flight Training Security Program. Type of Request: Extension of a currently approved collection. OMB Control Number: 1652-0021. Forms(s): N/A. Affected Public: As defined in 49 CFR 1552.3, candidates, flight training providers, and flight training provider employees. Abstract: This information collection relates to regulations issued by TSA for flight training providers and candidates. TSA conducts security threat assessments for aliens seeking flight training (``candidates'') from FAA-certified providers under 49 CFR part 1552. The information collection includes candidate biographic information and fingerprints which are used to determine whether the individual poses a threat to aviation or national security. The collection also includes flight training providers' creation of flight training events, designation and maintenance of security coordinators, and recordkeeping for required security awareness training for employees, including initial and biennial training retained for the duration of employment and at least 1 year afterward.
