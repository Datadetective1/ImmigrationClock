WHAT CHANGED THIS WEEK

[Major]
Board of Immigration Appeals precedent: 30 I&N Dec. 228 (BIA 2026)
Where a party offers an alternative proffer concerning a fact material to the bond determination or challenges a proffer as uncorroborated or inconsistent with information in the record, the Immigration Judge should address the alternative proffer or challenge and provide, in the bond memorandum, a reasoned explanation for the weight given to each account.
Agency: Executive Office for Immigration Review
Published: 2026-11-10
Read the official document: https://www.justice.gov/eoir/media/1471311/dl?inline

[Major]
Board of Immigration Appeals precedent: 30 I&N Dec. 236 (BIA 2026)
The proposed group of “Guatemalan women without partners” lacks particularity and social distinction and is therefore not a cognizable particular social group. An imputed opinion about social or societal conventions, without any relation to the government, does not qualify as an imputed political opinion. Matter of D-G-E-A- & N-G-G-E- , 29 I&N Dec. 570 (BIA 2026), reaffirmed.
Agency: Executive Office for Immigration Review
Published: 2026-11-12
Read the official document: https://www.justice.gov/eoir/media/1471516/dl?inline

COMING UP

  H-1B electronic registration - 2027-03-01
    USCIS opens the H-1B cap registration window in early March for at least 14 calendar days (about two weeks). Miss it and you wait a full year for the next lottery.
    USCIS: H-1B cap season: https://www.uscis.gov/working-in-the-united-states/temporary-workers/h-1b-specialty-occupations/h-1b-electronic-registration-process
  Federal tax filing deadline - 2027-04-15
    Individual returns are due April 15. This includes nonresident (1040-NR) returns for many F-1 and J-1 visa holders. File or request an extension by this date.
    IRS: Tax deadlines: https://www.irs.gov/filing
  New fiscal year: H-1B start date - 2027-10-01
    October 1 begins the federal fiscal year: cap-subject H-1B employment can start and a fresh set of visa numbers becomes available.
    USCIS: H-1B specialty occupations: https://www.uscis.gov/working-in-the-united-states/h-1b-specialty-occupations
  Diversity Visa (green-card) lottery - Dates set by the State Department · check the official page
    The State Department sets each year's entry period and announces it on its official DV page. It deferred the opening of the DV-2027 entry period, and entries now need a valid, unexpired passport and a $1 registration fee. Confirm current dates on the official page; no date is shown here until one is announced.
    U.S. Dept. of State: DV Program: https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/us-visas/immigrate/diversity-visa-program-entry.html

Dates published by the agency itself. They can move; the source link is the authority.
