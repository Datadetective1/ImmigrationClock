[Relevante] [Propuesta: no está en vigor]
Modernizing the Labor Market Test and Improving Protections for U.S. Workers in the PERM Immigrant Visa Program
The Department of Labor (Department or DOL) proposes to amend its regulations regarding the certification of job opportunities offered by employers seeking to employ aliens on a permanent basis in the United States. This Notice of Proposed Rulemaking (NPRM or proposed rule) strengthens the standards by which the Department receives and reviews employers' applications for permanent labor certification and improves the integrity of the overall employment-based immigrant visa program to more effectively protect the wages and job opportunities of U.S. workers similarly employed in the United States. Among the issues addressed in this proposed rule are eliminating further administration of Schedule A, which exempts certain job opportunities from the statutorily required labor market test; strengthening the minimum standards of recruitment to ensure all employers seeking labor certification access the broadest pool of high-quality U.S. workers using the latest technologies and methods; enhancing safeguards for U.S. workers impacted by layoffs, including those laid off by employers using third-party staffing companies and establishing standards for employers to report layoffs; expanding requirements for employers to provide U.S. workers with notice of filing (NOF) applications for permanent labor certification; improving employer compliance with program requirements and consideration of qualified U.S. workers that is comparable to job opportunities for which permanent labor certification is not required; and codifying the Department's practice of electronically receiving, processing, and issuing final determinations on employer requests for prevailing wages and permanent labor certifications. The changes in this proposed rule are consistent with the Secretary of Labor's (Secretary) statutory responsibilities to certify that there are not sufficient workers in the United States who are able, willing, qualified, and available to fill the petitioning employer's job opportunity at the time of application for a visa and admission to the United States, and that the employment of such alien will not adversely affect the wages and working conditions of similarly employed workers in the United States.
Agencia: U.S. Department of Labor

Board of Immigration Appeals precedent: 30 I&N Dec. 241 (BIA 2026)
Simply presenting hypothetical arguments and suggested alternative views of the evidence is not sufficient to establish clear error in the factual findings of the Immigration Judge. An appeal based merely on conjecture and speculation lacks an arguable basis in fact or in law, serves no purpose other than to create delay, and is legally inappropriate.

Appellate Procedures for the Board of Immigration Appeals
In February 2026, the Department issued an interim final rule ("IFR"), effective March 9, 2026, to streamline Board of Immigration Appeals ("Board" or "BIA") procedures. The IFR's comment period closed on April 8, 2026. In this final rule, the Department responds to comments received and finalizes the IFR with minor changes.

PRÓXIMAS FECHAS

  H-1B electronic registration - 2027-03-01
    USCIS opens the H-1B cap registration window in early March for at least 14 calendar days (about two weeks). Miss it and you wait a full year for the next lottery.
    USCIS: H-1B cap season: https://www.uscis.gov/working-in-the-united-states/temporary-workers/h-1b-specialty-occupations/h-1b-electronic-registration-process
  Federal tax filing deadline - 2027-04-15
    Individual returns are due April 15. This includes nonresident (1040-NR) returns for many F-1 and J-1 visa holders. File or request an extension by this date.
    IRS: Tax deadlines: https://www.irs.gov/filing
  New fiscal year: H-1B start date - 2027-10-01
    October 1 begins the federal fiscal year: cap-subject H-1B employment can start and a fresh set of visa numbers becomes available.
    USCIS: H-1B specialty occupations: https://www.uscis.gov/working-in-the-united-states/h-1b-specialty-occupations
  Diversity Visa (green-card) lottery - Dates set by the State Department · check the official page
    The State Department sets each year's entry period and announces it on its official DV page. It deferred the opening of the DV-2027 entry period, and entries now need a valid, unexpired passport and a $1 registration fee. Confirm current dates on the official page; no date is shown here until one is announced.
    U.S. Dept. of State: DV Program: https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/us-visas/immigrate/diversity-visa-program-entry.html
