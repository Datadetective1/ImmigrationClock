CE QUI A CHANGÉ CETTE SEMAINE

[Majeur]
Policy alert: Special Immigrant Juvenile Adjustment of Status
U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS) is updating policy guidance in the USCIS Policy Manual to address policy changes to Special Immigrant Juvenile (SIJ) based adjustment of status adjudications. The updates aim to ensure that all applicants who adjust status as an SIJ merit a favorable exercise of discretion, in addition to meeting all other eligibility requirements.
Agence: U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services
Publié le: 2026-11-20
Lire le document officiel: https://www.uscis.gov/sites/default/files/document/policy-manual-updates/20261120-SIJAdjustment.pdf

[Majeur]
Employee or Independent Contractor Status Under the Fair Labor Standards Act, Family and Medical Leave Act, and Migrant and Seasonal Agricultural Worker Protection Act
This final rule rescinds the analysis for determining employee or independent contractor status under the Fair Labor Standards Act (FLSA or Act) set forth in the Department of Labor's (Department) regulations and replaces it with an analysis similar to what it published in a final rule promulgated in 2021, with several modifications and enhancements. In addition, this final rule amends provisions in the Department's regulations implementing the Family and Medical Leave Act (FMLA) and Migrant and Seasonal Agricultural Worker Protection Act (MSPA) to provide that employee or independent contractor status under those laws should be determined using the Department's FLSA analysis, as the FMLA and MSPA both incorporate the FLSA's scope of employment.
Agence: U.S. Department of Labor
Publié le: 2026-11-23
Lire le document officiel: https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/11/23/2026-23777/employee-or-independent-contractor-status-under-the-fair-labor-standards-act-family-and-medical

[Majeur]
Board of Immigration Appeals precedent: 30 I&N Dec. 249 (BIA 2026)
Immigration Judges and the Board have no inherent equitable authority and thus lack authority to order or apply equitable remedies. The deadline for filing an appeal to the Board in 8 C.F.R. § 1003.38(b) is a nonjurisdictional, but mandatory, claims-processing rule and thus must be enforced if the opposing party properly invokes it. Matter of Morales-Morales , 28 I&N Dec. 714 (BIA 2023), overruled. The Board is not bound by circuit court decisions as precedent on issues that were not brought to the circuit court’s attention, considered, or ruled upon.
Agence: Executive Office for Immigration Review
Publié le: 2026-11-23
Lire le document officiel: https://www.justice.gov/eoir/media/1473971/dl?inline

À VENIR

  H-1B electronic registration - 2027-03-01
    USCIS opens the H-1B cap registration window in early March for at least 14 calendar days (about two weeks). Miss it and you wait a full year for the next lottery.
    USCIS: H-1B cap season: https://www.uscis.gov/working-in-the-united-states/temporary-workers/h-1b-specialty-occupations/h-1b-electronic-registration-process
  Federal tax filing deadline - 2027-04-15
    Individual returns are due April 15. This includes nonresident (1040-NR) returns for many F-1 and J-1 visa holders. File or request an extension by this date.
    IRS: Tax deadlines: https://www.irs.gov/filing
  New fiscal year: H-1B start date - 2027-10-01
    October 1 begins the federal fiscal year: cap-subject H-1B employment can start and a fresh set of visa numbers becomes available.
    USCIS: H-1B specialty occupations: https://www.uscis.gov/working-in-the-united-states/h-1b-specialty-occupations
  Diversity Visa (green-card) lottery - Dates set by the State Department · check the official page
    The State Department sets each year's entry period and announces it on its official DV page. It deferred the opening of the DV-2027 entry period, and entries now need a valid, unexpired passport and a $1 registration fee. Confirm current dates on the official page; no date is shown here until one is announced.
    U.S. Dept. of State: DV Program: https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/us-visas/immigrate/diversity-visa-program-entry.html
