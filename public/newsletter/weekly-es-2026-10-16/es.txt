[Relevante] [Propuesta — no está en vigor]
Removing Deference to Prior USCIS Determinations of Eligibility
The U.S. Department of Homeland Security (DHS), U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS), proposes to remove 8 CFR 214.1(c)(5), which generally provides that USCIS gives deference to a prior approval involving the same facts when adjudicating a request to extend a petition for an alien's employment or stay. This change would better align DHS regulations with the statute, which places the burden of proving eligibility for all immigration benefits on the petitioner, applicant, or beneficiary; strengthen adjudicatory review; and support agency priorities to improve the integrity of the immigration system and better protect the interests of U.S. workers.
Agencia: Department of Homeland Security
Publicado: 2026-10-14
Leer el documento oficial: https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/10/14/2026-20941/removing-deference-to-prior-uscis-determinations-of-eligibility

[Importante]
Removing 30-Day Refugee Termination Notice
This interim final rule (IFR) amends Department of Homeland Security (DHS) regulations to expedite termination of refugee status when DHS determines an alien was not a refugee within the meaning of section 101(a)(42) of the Immigration and Nationality Act at the time of admission to the United States. The response period to a Notice of Intent to Terminate (NOIT) is reduced from thirty to ten days. These changes aim to restore integrity to the immigration system, mitigate risks to national security and public safety, and prevent fraud and exploitation.
Agencia: Department of Homeland Security
Publicado: 2026-10-14
Leer el documento oficial: https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/10/14/2026-20956/removing-30-day-refugee-termination-notice

[Relevante]
Removal of Automatic Extensions for Certain Employment Authorization Documents
On October 30, 2025, DHS published an interim final rule (IFR) that amended DHS regulations to end the practice of automatically extending the validity of employment authorization documents (Forms I- 766 or EADs) for aliens who have timely filed an application to renew their EAD in certain employment authorization categories. This final rule responds to comments received on the IFR and reaffirms and finalizes the provisions of the IFR. Ending the automatic extension of EADs enhances benefit integrity and prioritizes the proper vetting and screening of aliens before granting a new period of employment authorization and/or a new EAD.
Agencia: Department of Homeland Security
Publicado: 2026-10-14
Leer el documento oficial: https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/10/14/2026-20955/removal-of-automatic-extensions-for-certain-employment-authorization-documents

[Relevante]
Imposition and Collection of Civil Penalties for Certain Immigration-Related Violations
On June 27, 2025, the Departments issued an interim final rule ("IFR") to streamline assessment of civil penalties under the Immigration and Nationality Act ("INA") for aliens who fail to depart during a voluntary departure period, willfully fail or refuse to depart after a final removal order and certain other proscribed activities, or are apprehended while improperly entering or attempting to enter the United States. This final rule addresses comments and finalizes the IFR with one change: it permits acceptance of a late-filed appeal based on documented mail delivery delays that ICE finds were beyond the alien's control.
Agencia: Department of Homeland Security
Publicado: 2026-10-14
Leer el documento oficial: https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/10/14/2026-21024/imposition-and-collection-of-civil-penalties-for-certain-immigration-related-violations

[Relevante]
Justice Department Moves to Strip Citizenship from 40 Criminals in Largest Single-Period Filing Under the Trump Administration
U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services partnered with the Department of Justice in its filing of denaturalization actions against dozens of individuals accused of serious offenses, including acting as an agent for a foreign government, committing voluntary manslaughter, and aggravated sodomy against a special‑needs man.
Agencia: U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services
Publicado: 2026-10-13
Leer el documento oficial: https://www.uscis.gov/newsroom/news-releases/justice-department-moves-to-strip-citizenship-from-40-criminals-in-largest-single-period-filing

PRÓXIMAS FECHAS

  H-1B electronic registration - 2027-03-01
    USCIS opens the H-1B cap registration window in early March for at least 14 calendar days — about two weeks. Miss it and you wait a full year for the next lottery.
    USCIS — H-1B cap season: https://www.uscis.gov/working-in-the-united-states/temporary-workers/h-1b-specialty-occupations/h-1b-electronic-registration-process
  Federal tax filing deadline - 2027-04-15
    Individual returns are due April 15 — including nonresident (1040-NR) returns for many F-1 and J-1 visa holders. File or request an extension by this date.
    IRS — Tax deadlines: https://www.irs.gov/filing
  Diversity Visa (green-card) lottery - 2027-10-01
    The DV lottery registration opens for about one month in the fall, free, for nationals of eligible countries. A rare path to a green card with no employer or family sponsor.
    U.S. Dept. of State — DV Program: https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/us-visas/immigrate/diversity-visa-program-entry.html
  New fiscal year — H-1B start date - 2027-10-01
    October 1 begins the federal fiscal year: cap-subject H-1B employment can start and a fresh set of visa numbers becomes available.
    USCIS — H-1B specialty occupations: https://www.uscis.gov/working-in-the-united-states/h-1b-specialty-occupations
