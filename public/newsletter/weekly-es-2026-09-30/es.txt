LO QUE CAMBIÓ ESTA SEMANA

[Importante]
U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services Employment-Based Immigrant Visa, Fifth Preference (EB-5) Fee Rule
This final rule adjusts the Employment-Based Immigration, Fifth Preference (EB-5) immigration benefit request fees charged by U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS). It also codifies provisions of the EB-5 Reform and Integrity Act of 2022, implements new statutory requirements, and addresses public comments received on the proposed fee rule published on October 23, 2025.
Agencia: Department of Homeland Security
Publicado: 2026-09-30
Leer el documento oficial: https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/09/30/2026-20016/us-citizenship-and-immigration-services-employment-based-immigrant-visa-fifth-preference-eb-5-fee

[Relevante]
USCIS Announces FY 2027 Inflation Increase for Certain Immigration-Related Fees
U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services has issued a&nbsp;Federal Register notice&nbsp;that will increase certain H.R. 1 immigration-related fees for fiscal year 2027.
Agencia: U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services
Publicado: 2026-09-30
Leer el documento oficial: https://www.uscis.gov/newsroom/alerts/uscis-announces-fy-2027-inflation-increase-for-certain-immigration-related-fees

[Relevante]
USCIS Adjusts EB-5 Fees to Cover Program Costs and Strengthen Integrity Measures
U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS) is updating fees for the EB-5 Immigrant Investor Program to&nbsp;ensure EB-5 fees fully recover the cost of operating the program, meet statutory processing time goals, and support integrity measures such as audits and site visits.
Agencia: U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services
Publicado: 2026-09-29
Leer el documento oficial: https://www.uscis.gov/newsroom/alerts/uscis-adjusts-eb-5-fees-to-cover-program-costs-and-strengthen-integrity-measures

[Relevante]
Policy alert: Controlled Substance Trafficking, Significant Trafficking in Persons, and Money Laundering Grounds of Inadmissibility
U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS) is issuing policy guidance in the USCIS Policy Manual to address the inadmissibility of aliens engaged in controlled substance trafficking, significant trafficking in persons, and money laundering under section 212(a)(2) of the Immigration and Nationality Act (INA).
Agencia: U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services
Publicado: 2026-09-23
Leer el documento oficial: https://www.uscis.gov/sites/default/files/document/policy-manual-updates/20260923-CriminalInadmissibility.pdf

[Relevante]
Policy alert: Definition of Day for Purposes of Filing Deadlines
U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS) is issuing policy guidance in the USCIS Policy Manual to clarify how USCIS applies the regulatory definition of “day” to filing deadlines. This policy becomes effective October 23, 2026.
Agencia: U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services
Publicado: 2026-09-23
Leer el documento oficial: https://www.uscis.gov/sites/default/files/document/policy-manual-updates/20260923-DefinitionOfDay.pdf

PRÓXIMAS FECHAS

  Diversity Visa (green-card) lottery - 2026-10-01
    The DV lottery registration opens for about one month in the fall, free, for nationals of eligible countries. A rare path to a green card with no employer or family sponsor.
    U.S. Dept. of State — DV Program: https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/us-visas/immigrate/diversity-visa-program-entry.html
  New fiscal year — H-1B start date - 2026-10-01
    October 1 begins the federal fiscal year: cap-subject H-1B employment can start and a fresh set of visa numbers becomes available.
    USCIS — H-1B specialty occupations: https://www.uscis.gov/working-in-the-united-states/h-1b-specialty-occupations
  H-1B electronic registration - 2027-03-01
    USCIS opens the H-1B cap registration window in early March for at least 14 calendar days — about two weeks. Miss it and you wait a full year for the next lottery.
    USCIS — H-1B cap season: https://www.uscis.gov/working-in-the-united-states/temporary-workers/h-1b-specialty-occupations/h-1b-electronic-registration-process
  Federal tax filing deadline - 2027-04-15
    Individual returns are due April 15 — including nonresident (1040-NR) returns for many F-1 and J-1 visa holders. File or request an extension by this date.
    IRS — Tax deadlines: https://www.irs.gov/filing
