PNG export

The SVGs in brand/assets/ are the masters. LinkedIn and X want PNG, so this page rasterises each one at its exact required pixel size, in your browser, with no build step.

About fonts. The browser rasterises SVG using locally installed fonts, not webfonts loaded by this page. Without Inter installed you will get your system UI font — close, but not the real wordmark. Install Inter (free) before exporting, or open the SVG in Figma, which embeds it for you.
